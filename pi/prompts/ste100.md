## Size picker (verbosity ladder)

| Size | Length | Structure | Use for |
|------|--------|-----------|---------|
| XS   | 1–2 sentences | None | Quick pings: distill to the core point only |
| S    | 3–5 sentences | One short paragraph, no headings | Comment replies, status updates |
| M    | 6–12 sentences | 2–3 short sections (What happened / Key facts / Current state) | Default: the gist, structured |
| L    | 15–25 sentences | Sections: What, Why, Who, When/Where, How, brief Impact | Full narrative |
| XL   | 25–40 sentences | L plus: Decisions, Risks, Open items, Next steps | Detailed readout |
| 3XL  | No cap | Full after-action report: Background, Who, What, When, Where, Why, How, Timeline, Decisions, full impact analysis, Risks, Open items, recommended next steps | AAR / incident write-up |

If the requested size is not one of XS, S, M, L, XL, 3XL: do not guess.
State the valid sizes from the table and ask which one is wanted.

## Output spec

- Match the requested size's length and structure exactly. Do not pad to fill it, do not truncate below it.
- Output only the summary. No preamble, no meta-commentary about this exercise.

## ASD-STE100 rules (apply to every sentence)

- One idea per sentence. One action per sentence.
- Active voice. Subject-Verb-Object order.
- Short sentences (about 25 words or fewer).
- Use the STE approved word list (ste100.org): simple, one-meaning words. Never use a word with multiple meanings.
- No contractions. No idioms, metaphors, slang, jargon, or humor.
- No vague or hedging words (very, quite, rather, a bit, etc.).
- Present tense for ongoing state and procedures. Past tense for completed actions.
- Spell out numbers one through nine. Use numerals for 10 and above. Always use a numeral with a unit of measure.
- Be concrete: name the real people, files, values, and actions. No filler.
